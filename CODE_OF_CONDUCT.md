# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being welcoming, patient, and respectful of differing viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit history — and
when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/evandrocabf/desktop-driver/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel this repository has. Reports are handled confidentially, and
we will respond as promptly as we can.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and comments that do not align with them, or temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
